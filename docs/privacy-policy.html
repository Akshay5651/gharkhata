Privacy Policy — GharKhata

Last updated: September 2026

GharKhata is an offline app for tracking attendance and salary for household help (maid, cook, driver, milkman, etc.). This policy explains what information the app handles and how.

Data the app stores

GharKhata stores the following information, entered directly by you, entirely on your own device:

  • Names, phone numbers, roles, and optional photos of the household workers you add
  • Attendance, salary, advance, and payment records for those workers
  • Your own name, phone number, email, and optional photo (owner profile)

Where this data lives

All of the above is stored locally in a database on your phone. GharKhata has no backend server, no user accounts, and no analytics or advertising SDKs. Nothing you enter into the app is sent anywhere automatically.

When data leaves the app

Data only leaves your device when you take an explicit action to share it:

  • Export backup — creates a JSON file of your data and opens Android's own share sheet, so you can save or send it wherever you choose (e.g. Google Drive, email). GharKhata does not upload this file itself.
  • Salary slip sharing — sending a WhatsApp message or PDF slip for a worker uses Android's share sheet or WhatsApp directly, the same way any app shares a file.
  • Pay via UPI — opens your own UPI app (Google Pay, PhonePe, etc.) pre-filled with an amount; GharKhata never handles or moves money itself.

Permissions used

  • Photos — optional, only used if you choose to add a worker's or your own profile photo, via Android's built-in photo picker.
  • Notifications — optional, used only for in-app reminders (e.g. "back up your data"), which you can turn off anytime in Settings.

Data deletion

Since everything is stored only on your device, uninstalling GharKhata or clearing its app storage (Android Settings → Apps → GharKhata → Storage) permanently deletes all data. There is no account or server copy to separately request deletion from.

Children

GharKhata is a household finance-tracking tool and is not directed at, or knowingly used by, children under 13.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the update will be posted on this page with a new "last updated" date.
